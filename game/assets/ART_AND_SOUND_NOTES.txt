RE:TRACE Prototype 9 — additional changes

B6/B5/B4/B3 backgrounds were edited with built-in image generation. Exact
camera, geometry, device positions, silhouettes and lighting were constrained.
The additions are accumulated dust, old water/rust trails below their sources,
scuffs, papers and fallen plaster clustered around plausible origins. B6 has
the heaviest damage, B5 clear abandonment, B4 less damage with neglected work
areas, and B3 modest wear. The accepted B2 and B1 artwork is unchanged.
The full per-floor prompt text is in ART_EDIT_PROMPTS.md in the project root.

sfx_lever_reset.wav reuses this game's original synthesized lever clunk with a
75 ms lead so it follows the sensor beep. It plays only on an actual on-to-off
change, caused by either B3 room door. The short original sensor beep is made
by the existing make_sound function at initialization. Staying on the sensor
never repeats either cue. Active echoes use the quieter replay volume.
The optional offline renderer includes the delayed lever cue for rebuilding.

RE:TRACE Prototype 8 — asset and authorship notes

The user established the project concept, selected the visual/music direction,
designed and reviewed puzzles collaboratively, and supplied repeated playtest
feedback. The assistant implemented this revision, edited the artwork with
built-in image generation, and arranged/rendered the music and effects.

ROOM ART
Six existing backgrounds were edited, preserving framing, elevated frontal
camera, every wall/device/doorway/cable position and collision geometry.
The common instruction was: remove orphan glossy floor reflection streaks,
keep only short localized illumination attributable to visible lamps/devices,
use matte readable grey floors, and keep early elevator interiors dim.
No characters, labels, new devices, obstacles or room redesigns were requested.

Per-floor edit instructions:
B6: heaviest neglect, peeling plaster, old leaks, grime, existing rubble; dim
    desaturated amber fixtures, preserve the luminous blue observation window.
B5: pronounced dust/rust/staining and peeling paint; softer amber fixtures;
    remove both long orange floor stripes and retain the existing clutter.
B4: moderate grime/scuffs and mild rust; muted amber; remove the unsupported
    right-wall glow and all floor streaks across/below the partitions.
B3: maintained equipment with residual old wall-base wear; white fixtures;
    remove long white streaks, especially below the archive shutters.
B2: clean operational room, repaired peeling paint, very little grime;
    steady white fixtures, no streaks below the service enclosure.
B1: nearly spotless pale clinical room; matte floor, no disconnected bright
    patches. The game supplies the final warm doorway light dynamically.

b1_gate_panels.png is a separate texture edit of elevator.png: neutral matte
silver leaf surfaces with the original geometry, minus orange/blue reflections.
It is used only for internal B1 shutters; the cinematic elevator image and
closing animation remain unchanged. All earlier assets remain in Prototype 7.

SOUND
Glass Echo is an original note arrangement made for RE:TRACE, based on the B
sample selected by the user. The basement uses a 120-second variation cycle;
the matching filtered file supplies the elevator mix. Surface Release is a
72-second brighter D-major variation. Note events and renderer are included
in audio_source. Instrument timbres use the TimGM6mb SoundFont through
FluidSynth, not recordings made by the assistant. The bank and its licence
and copyright notice are included in audio_source.

All new device effects are original synthesized sounds, including the generic
three-note descending shutdown. They do not sample a phone brand's sound.
The indoor machinery layer is a tonal electrical hum, not filtered wind.
outside_wind.wav retains the previous original synthesized outdoor ambience.
The runtime uses only pygame; offline music-generation dependencies are not
needed by players. Target-device listening remains part of the playtest.

Bundled font licences are provided separately in this assets folder.
